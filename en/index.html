about me

PROFE SABRI

I'm Sabrina Goldenstein, a Physical Education teacher from Buenos Aires. I spent my childhood in gyms, tagging along with my father — Master Jorge Goldenstein, founder of the Shaolin Lao Hu Kung Fu School. As a teenager I was already teaching kids' classes and helping out in the adult ones. Movement has always been my world.

Over the years I kept widening my path: from working with kids to adult training, strength training and high performance. I kept studying because I always felt there was more to learn.

  • Physical Education Teacher (Profesorado Superior) — ISEF No. 1 Dr. Enrique Romero Brest
  • Bachelor's in High-Performance Sport — Univ. of Lomas de Zamora
  • Lifeguard — Argentina (EPSA) · Brazil · USA (Red Cross)
  • BLS Instructor (EPSA) · Lifeguard Instructor (Red Cross)
  • Kung Fu Instructor — Shaolin Lao Hu School
🇦🇷 Español 🇺🇸 English 🇧🇷 Português
MY APPROACH
When I train anyone, my north star is always health. Whatever goal you start with — gaining muscle mass, improving a specific physical capacity, a particular aesthetic goal — that north star is never lost. Maintaining and improving health in a holistic way leads to a better quality of life, a longer life expectancy and a wider margin to achieve any goal.
